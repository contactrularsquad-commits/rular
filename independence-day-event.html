Rular Squad's First Independence Day Community Initiative

15 August 2026 · Organised by Rular Squad Foundation · Malipur, Uttar Pradesh, India

View Photos Watch Vlog
Celebrating Independence Through Community

A day about responsibility as much as celebration

On 15 August 2026, Rular Squad Foundation organised its first Independence Day community initiative with the aim of bringing young people and children together around the values of responsibility, education, unity and service.

The initiative was not only about celebrating Independence Day, but also about encouraging the younger generation to understand that building a better community begins with participation and responsibility.

📚 Supporting Children

Copies, notebooks and pens for every child

As part of the initiative, Rular Squad Foundation provided copies/notebooks and pens to children. The purpose was to encourage children towards education and remind them that even a small contribution can become meaningful when a community comes together.
