Who We Are

Real change begins at the local level.

Rular Squad is a community-driven initiative started with the idea that real change begins at the local level. Instead of waiting for someone else to improve our surroundings, Rular Squad encourages young people and community members to come together and take responsibility for their village.

🌳

Our Mission

To bring the people of our village together — especially the youth.

To transform community participation into meaningful action for a cleaner, greener, educated, united and progressive village.

Youth Leadership

Building confidence and ownership among young volunteers.

Environment

Plantation and green-cover initiatives across Malipur.

Cleanliness

Regular drives to keep public spaces clean and safe.

Education

Access to books, materials and mentoring for children.

Community Activities

Events that bring generations of the village together.

Martyr Remembrance

Preserving the legacy of Malipur's own heroes.

Social Awareness

Conversations that shift how the village sees its own future.

Skills & Volunteering

Structured ways for anyone to contribute their ability.

Our Vision

A model village where every generation participates.

To build a model village community where every generation participates in development and every individual feels responsible for the future of the village.

The Idea

Many villages have talented young people but often lack a platform where they can work together. Rular Squad aims to create that platform.

One person can start a change. A team can create a movement. A united village can create a future.
